# Emotional Profiles Summary

**Coverage gap, up front:** only 36 of 109 transcripts (33%) carry one of these 8 defined archetype slugs. The other 67% either have no archetype recorded in these files or use a different, free-text psychology label — e.g. R2R.json's own `most_common_emotional_profiles` lists "growth-oriented-investor" and "pragmatic-cost-focused", names outside this 8-slug taxonomy entirely. Read the 4 archetypes below as real but partial; don't treat their conversion rates as representative of the full 109.

## analytical-evaluator (n=17, conversion rate 0.5)
- Focuses on: income-figure provenance/forecast reliability (7); remote/abroad management logistics & operational track record (5); fee/cost breakdown transparency (5)
- Moves them: real named comparables/case-study data over assertion (4); concrete operational specifics that address remote-management trust (4); a forecast-vs-actual case study shown at the exact moment confidence is questioned (2, new pattern this run)
- Falls flat: the analyser tool visibly struggling with atypical properties, e.g. 5-bed cap or misread bedroom count (2); percentage/relative fee framing that reads as income eroding away (1)
- Offer framing: flexible/extendable, never a hard countdown. 6 offers made to this archetype, 0% accepted.
- Delays/objections: DLY-OTH dominant (15 tags/14 entries — most carry more than one delay), then DLY-FUR (5), DLY-PUR (4). New this run: anchoring a worst-case STL figure directly against a lead's own known current income (HMO/student rent) collapsed a credibility/guarantee objection twice (Paresh, Afsar).

## certainty-seeker (n=11, conversion rate 0.1 — lowest of the 4)
- Focuses on: worst-case/floor income still covering mortgage+bills (8); hands-off/remote management logistics (5); contract exit/notice terms (5)
- Moves them: explicit worst-case percentage-off arithmetic delivered as a direct, confident line — "even if I'm 50% off, you're still doing a thousand pounds of profit" (4, strengthened this run); a real, honest case study or named local comparable (4)
- Falls flat: promised follow-ups left undelivered — a callback, a number, a check (3); no firm next-meeting date locked in, letting momentum die (3)
- Offer framing: anchor any discount to a real external deadline (mortgage switch, lease renewal) — manufactured urgency reads as pressure.
- Delays/objections: DLY-TEN, DLY-FUR, DLY-MOV common; DLY-OTH appears in most entries (often "wants a second conversation before committing," or this run, awaiting probate). Top objections: company-trust skepticism (0% resolution) and guaranteed-rent-elsewhere comparisons (67% resolution).

## fast-path-gain-focused (n=2, conversion rate 1.0 — both entries accepted an offer at the meeting)
- Focuses on: revenue vs current position; fee/cleaning costs and onboarding timeline (each freq 2 now); local track record/credibility for a remote/absentee owner (new this run)
- Moves them: a discounted management fee offered and applied live at the meeting — now 2 of 2 recorded offers to this archetype accepted; local credibility confirmed briefly without an extended trust pitch closed the second case (entry 086, Kabir) in an 8-minute call
- Falls flat: nothing recorded (n still small)
- Offer framing: immediate and meeting-exclusive — proactively offer a bundle/multi-property discount as soon as a lead signals wanting more than one property, do not wait to be asked.
- Delays/objections: no delay recorded in either entry (has_delay=False both times).

## loss-averse-switcher (n=6, conversion rate 0.4)
- Focuses on: fully hands-off management, given they don't want more to manage (4, now top concern); direct £-uplift vs current long-let rent/threshold (3); front-loaded furnishing/setup spend weighed against a modest income uplift (new this run)
- Moves them: reframing furnishing/setup spend as a payback/ROI figure — e.g. "an extra £400/month is a ~100% return in Year 1" (new pattern, entry 089); a concrete £-uplift that clearly beats their current rent or stated threshold (3)
- Falls flat: an absent co-decision-maker (partner/spouse) left unengaged on the call (2); furnishing/setup cost left as a vague range at the point of the offer (2)
- Offer framing: protection against a known, already-experienced cost (bad tenant, empty property, double council tax) — not speculative upside. 1 offer made to this archetype, 0% accepted.
- Delays/objections: DLY-FUR, DLY-TEN, DLY-MOV common — but the more decisive blocker is often an absent co-decision-maker rather than a coded delay. Green lights split evenly: GL-TIME/GL-MGMT/GL-SELF.

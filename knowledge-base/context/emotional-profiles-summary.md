# Emotional Profiles Summary

**Coverage gap, up front:** only 30 of 103 transcripts (29%) carry one of these 8 defined archetype slugs. The other 71% either have no archetype recorded in these files or use a different, free-text psychology label — e.g. R2R.json's own `most_common_emotional_profiles` lists "growth-oriented-investor" and "pragmatic-cost-focused", names outside this 8-slug taxonomy entirely. Read the 4 archetypes below as real but partial; don't treat their conversion rates as representative of the full 103.

## analytical-evaluator (n=14, conversion rate 0.5)
- Focuses on: income-figure provenance/forecast reliability (5); remote/abroad management logistics & operational track record (5); independently verifying figures against their own spreadsheet or a competing quote (3)
- Moves them: real named comparables/case-study data over assertion (4); concrete operational specifics that address remote-management trust (4); honest disclosure of ramp-up/downside before it's asked (3)
- Falls flat: the analyser tool visibly struggling with atypical properties, e.g. 5-bed cap or misread bedroom count (2); percentage/relative fee framing that reads as income eroding away (1)
- Offer framing: flexible/extendable, never a hard countdown — a ticking-clock offer reads as pressure to a lead already convinced by data. 6 offers made to this archetype, 0% accepted.
- Delays/objections: DLY-OTH dominant (15 tags/14 entries — most carry more than one delay), then DLY-FUR (5), DLY-PUR (4). Top objection: skepticism that income figures are inflated (freq 3, 100% resolution when methodology is explained).

## certainty-seeker (n=10, conversion rate 0.1 — lowest of the 4)
- Focuses on: worst-case/floor income still covering mortgage+bills (7); hands-off/remote management logistics (5); contract exit/notice terms (4)
- Moves them: a real, honest case study or named local comparable (4); worst-case-month arithmetic proving costs are covered (3); concrete operational-security mechanics, not vague reassurance (3)
- Falls flat: promised follow-ups left undelivered — a callback, a number, a check (3); no firm next-meeting date locked in, letting momentum die (3)
- Offer framing: anchor any discount to a real external deadline (mortgage switch, lease renewal) — manufactured urgency reads as pressure.
- Delays/objections: DLY-TEN, DLY-FUR, DLY-MOV common; DLY-OTH appears in 7/10 (often "wants a second conversation before committing"). Top objections: company-trust skepticism (freq 3, 0% resolution — hardest to shift) and guaranteed-rent-elsewhere comparisons (freq 3, 67% resolution).

## fast-path-gain-focused (n=1 — directional only, do not generalise)
- Focuses on: revenue vs current position, fee/cleaning costs, guest vetting, onboarding timeline (each freq 1)
- Moves them: a discounted management fee offered and applied live at the meeting — the only recorded accepted offer in the entire 103-transcript dataset; revenue shown directly against current position; both decision-makers present and aligned
- Falls flat: nothing recorded (n too small)
- Offer framing: immediate and meeting-exclusive, not something to think over — this is the one archetype where speed, not deferral, converted.
- Delays/objections: no delay recorded (has_delay=False). Single green light: GL-TIME (property already on market, income needed now).

## loss-averse-switcher (n=5, conversion rate 0.4)
- Focuses on: direct £-uplift vs current long-let rent/threshold (3); fully hands-off management, given they don't want more to manage (3); avoiding long-let tenant risk after a bad prior experience (2)
- Moves them: a concrete £-uplift that clearly beats their current rent or stated threshold (3); guest-vetting/damage-protection specifics speaking directly to prior trauma (2); local case studies or comparable-property proof (2)
- Falls flat: an absent co-decision-maker (partner/spouse) left unengaged on the call (2); furnishing/setup cost left as a vague range at the point of the offer (2)
- Offer framing: protection against a known, already-experienced cost (bad tenant, empty property, double council tax) — not speculative upside. 1 offer made to this archetype, 0% accepted.
- Delays/objections: DLY-FUR, DLY-TEN, DLY-MOV common — but the more decisive blocker is often an absent co-decision-maker rather than a coded delay. Green lights split evenly: GL-TIME/GL-MGMT/GL-SELF (3/5 each).

# Stayful Presentation Guide

**Training confidence: strong** — 103 transcripts analysed; profile-type detail is good–strong for STL-SW/EX-STL/PURCH/ABROAD (17–37 entries each) and building for SELL/R2R (6–8 entries each).

---

## Sections That Land Well vs. Create Friction

**Lands well:**
- A worst-case/floor monthly figure walked through live, arithmetic shown, rather than an average headline number stated and left unexplained.
- Naming the accuracy methodology (comparable count, radius, inputs) when a figure is challenged, rather than reasserting the number.
- Volunteering the ramp-up/downside disclosure (year-one runs 15–20% below full potential, 12–18 months to full run-rate) *before* it's asked.
- Concrete operational mechanics — deposit amount, insurance ceiling, spend-notification threshold — in place of general reassurance on damage/guest-quality worries.
- Honest concessions where the honest answer doesn't favour Stayful (self-furnishing might be cheaper; a competing quote's uplift genuinely is thin) — these consistently land as trust-building rather than sales-damaging.

**Creates friction (from do-not-say.json's 46 flagged moments):**
- A live concern left only partially resolved before moving to the next topic (14 of 46 flagged moments — the largest single friction category).
- Figures delivered inconsistently or misspoken (6) — a worst-month figure stated wrong then corrected, or the analyser tool visibly misreading inputs live.
- Technical failures — screen share not working, audio dropping — derailing the flow before substantive content is reached (7).
- A flat, under-informed answer to a direct ask with no immediate alternative offered (3) — most damaging when it's the very first response to a guaranteed-income or funding-viability question.
- An automated tool (the website assessment tool, an auto-generated presentation) contradicting what's being said live (2) — undermines the whole pitch's credibility, not just the one figure.

## Optimal Presentation Order (from each profile-type file's `presentation_section_priority`)

There is no single universal order — the right sequence depends on profile type, but a consistent shape emerges: **name the specific trigger/pain → income figures (worst-case-inclusive) → operational/logistics reassurance → resolve practical blockers → dated close.** Profile-specific detail:

- **STL-SW**: name the specific pain driving them away from the current arrangement → worst-case-inclusive net income vs. their actual current/available LTL return → confirm hands-off management early → guest-vetting/protection mechanics → resolve furnishing/mortgage/council-tax blockers with firm figures → close on a dated next step tied to whatever external event already gates their timeline.
- **EX-STL**: diagnose specifically why the current setup underperforms, using their own numbers → contrast Stayful point-by-point against the current company (fee, platforms, screening, reporting cadence, individual vs. shared listing) → income with an honest worst-case/average range and in-hand proof points → transition/handover mechanics → contract terms, scale angle if relevant, deadline-bound offer.
- **PURCH**: establish the purchase/completion timeline and any financing dependency first, since it structurally gates everything else → income with honest ramp-up framing and real local comparables, not just best-case → full cost picture (fees, bills, setup) against the mortgage → contract flexibility and remote-ownership reassurance → property-specific configuration/guest-profile questions → dated close tied to the completion milestone.
- **SELL**: name the cost of the property sitting empty/underperforming → confirm STL and an active sale listing are fully compatible (calendar access, viewings, easy exit) → net STL income clearly covering mortgage/running costs, framed as reducing outgoings not maximising profit → operational reassurance (insurance, damage, cleaning, council tax) → deadline-bound discounted offer paired with a same-day finalised furnishing quote.
- **ABROAD**: lead with the fully-remote operational model (key access, guest vetting, maintenance thresholds, communication cadence) *before* figures — "how does this work when I'm not here" is this profile's dominant unspoken question → month-by-month STL vs. LTL comparison, not just an annual headline → calendar-blocking flexibility for return visits → legal/consent blockers resolved explicitly → close aligned to the actual departure date.
- **R2R**: confirm deal structure and rent level first — this is the hard viability gate for every R2R lead → real location-specific comparable STL revenue, not deal-source figures → full layered cost math (rent + fee + setup), including honest advice to walk away if it doesn't work → only then cover the management model itself → property-type compliance screening (HMO/shared accommodation) early to avoid wasted time.

## Figures That Get Challenged (income-related objection clusters, from objections.json)

| Challenge | Freq | Resolution rate | What resolved it |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Worst month is really that low?" (STL projection stress-test) | 2 | 0% | Framed as 1-in-12 against the full-year average; be honest the LTL comparison figure is often aspirational and carries its own void risk |
| R2R margins too thin after rent + fees | 2 | 0% | Present the layered math transparently — this challenge does not resolve through better framing, only through a genuinely different deal |
| "You said the revenue is double what I actually make" | 1 | 100% | Asked about review count/listing specifics; identified a ~1-year review gap as the likely explanation rather than disputing the lead's own figure |
| "The income kind of disappears... how do I know we won't end up making losses?" | 1 | 0% | Walked through actual current costs and mortgage — left unresolved in this instance; flagged honestly as a genuine open gap, not a scripted save |
| Skepticism the figures are inflated / generic | 1 | 100% | Named the methodology directly — comparable count out to a stated radius (e.g. "gone to a maximum of around 10km of local competition") |
| Fee negotiation anchored to a cheaper long-let rate | 1 | 0% | "There's quite a lot to do versus regular letting" — did not resolve on the call; no stronger recorded counter exists in the current dataset |
| General income credibility ("are you sure about that?") | 1 | 100% | Cited the ~10% accuracy range directly: "it's very rare I'll have it below 10%" |

Two of these seven challenges (margins-too-thin, fee-anchored-to-LTL-rate) currently have **no recorded successful resolution** in the dataset — flag honestly rather than presenting a scripted answer that hasn't actually worked yet.

## By Profile Type — What to Lead With

- **STL-SW**: name the specific pain (rising mortgage, bad tenant, empty/underperforming property) and mirror it back directly, then a worst-case-inclusive net figure against their actual current/available LTL return.
- **EX-STL**: skip re-pitching STL as a concept — they already believe in it. Lead with a specific, evidenced diagnosis of why the current setup underperforms, then contrast Stayful point-by-point against the current company.
- **PURCH**: establish the purchase/completion timeline and financing dependency first, since it structurally gates everything else, then present figures with honest ramp-up framing and real local comparables.
- **SELL**: open by naming the cost of the property sitting empty or underperforming, then immediately reassure that STL and an active sale listing are fully compatible.
- **ABROAD**: lead with the fully-remote operational model before figures — certainty on "how does this actually work" unlocks genuine engagement with the numbers.
- **R2R**: lead with an honest, transparent margin calculation before anything else — R2R leads live or die entirely on whether the numbers stack up.

## By Archetype — What Lands Best

- **certainty-seeker** (10 entries): worst-case-month arithmetic proving costs are still covered (freq 3); a real, honest case study or named local comparable (freq 4); concrete operational security mechanics (freq 3); honest ramp-up disclosure volunteered unprompted (freq 3).
- **loss-averse-switcher** (5 entries): a concrete £-uplift figure that clearly beats their current rent or stated threshold (freq 3); guest-vetting/damage specifics speaking directly to prior trauma (freq 2); local case studies or comparable-property proof (freq 2).
- **analytical-evaluator** (14 entries): real, named comparable or case-study data proving figures rather than asserting them (freq 4); concrete operational specifics addressing abroad/remote trust (freq 4); honest disclosure of ramp-up/downside before it's asked (freq 3); Zac openly conceding or validating the lead's own research (freq 3).
- **fast-path-gain-focused** (1 entry only — indicative, not validated): a special/discounted fee offered live at the meeting; revenue projections shown directly against current position; both decision-makers present and aligned.
- **urgency-driven, status-quo-resistant, betrayal-damaged, social-proof-dependent**: **zero logged entries.** No "what lands best" pattern can be honestly stated for these four archetypes from the current dataset.
